Questions

When should I use this site?

At the very end. Finish your full edit first, picture and sound together, and export it. Prepare that export here, then upload the saved file to TikTok without changing anything, either in another app or inside TikTok.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own device. The page never sends your video to a server.

Does Prepare change how my video looks?

No. Prepare keeps your picture exactly as exported. Only Enhance changes the picture, and only when you choose it.

What does Enhance do?

It rebuilds the clip at 1080p with high-quality resampling, restores edge detail with adaptive sharpening, and corrects dull or washed-out color only when the clip needs it. It cannot recover detail that was never recorded.

Why did TikTok still compress my video?

Usually because something was changed after preparing: editing the saved file in another app, or adding TikTok sounds, music, filters, text or trims inside TikTok. Any of these makes TikTok build a new file. A sound track that goes completely silent at the very end can also stop it working. TikTok can change how it handles uploads at any time.

Which browsers work?

Prepare works in any modern browser. Enhance needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari and runs on your graphics chip, so a computer is much faster than a phone.

What files can I use?

MP4 or MOV with AAC sound, the default for CapCut and phone cameras. iPhone "High Efficiency" (HEVC) clips can be enhanced where your browser can play them.
